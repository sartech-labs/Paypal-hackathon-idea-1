      Connect your domain

      In production these steps put SARA in front of your real traffic. This demo skips them: the playground sends its test agents to the gateway directly.

      1. Verify ownershipAdd a TXT record sara-verify=<token> to your domain. Until it is found, the gateway does not charge agents for this site.
        Skipped in this demo
      2. Point the domain at SARAChange the DNS record (or your CDN's origin) for your domain to the gateway. SARA forwards people and paying agents to your origin server.
        Skipped in this demo
      3. Lock the originAccept only requests that carry SARA's secret X-SARA-Origin-Key header, so agents cannot reach the origin directly.
        Skipped in this demo
