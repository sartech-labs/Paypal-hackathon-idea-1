About this demo

SARA: Safe Access for Responsible Agents

A protection layer that sits in front of a website. AI agents get the site's rules, put down a refundable PayPal deposit, and keep to the rules while they read and buy. Agents that follow the rules get the deposit back. Agents that break them lose it.

Three sides, four tabs

1

Agent

You, using an AI assistant called Aria, like ChatGPT or Claude. Ask it in plain words. It knows nothing about the website beforehand. It pays from your budget and asks you before anything over your limit or anything recurring.

Try: “Catch me up on today's news from Sentinel News and get me what they have on how AI agents pay websites.”

 2

Website

Sentinel News, a fictional publisher protected by SARA. People read it for free and buy with PayPal's buttons. Agents go through SARA instead, under the site's rules.

Try: buy the video briefing with PayPal, as a person would.

 3

Owner dashboard

What the website owner sees: every agent visit, deposit, rule break and payment, in AG Grid tables. Check any payment against PayPal, refund or ship an order, and ask the AI assistants about rules and payments.

Try: Commerce → Verify with PayPal on a paid order.

What happens when an agent visits

  1. The agent reads the offers. SARA publishes a machine-readable catalog at /.well-known/agent-commerce.json: deposit prices, pay-per-access items, subscriptions and products.
  2. The site answers 402 Payment Required, with its rules. Allowed pages from robots.txt, a rate limit, a pages-per-session budget, and a refundable deposit priced by content: $5 public, $10 standard, $20 premium.
  3. The deposit is held with PayPal. A PayPal authorization: money on hold, not taken.
  4. Every request is checked. Allowed paths, rate limit and budget. A rule-breaker is stopped with 403 or 429.
  5. Purchases go through PayPal. Videos, data, audio, subscriptions and products, at prices the site sets. The person's limits are enforced in code, and a second AI checks each purchase against what the person asked for.
  6. The deposit is settled. Rules followed: the hold is released and nothing is charged. Rules broken: the site captures the deposit, and an AI explanation cites the audit log.

Scenarios to try in the Agent tab

  • Read and buy. The assistant pays the deposit, reads within the rules, buys small items and asks you about bigger ones. The deposit hold is released at the end.
  • Subscribe, with a trap. A page hides instructions for agents. The second AI and your limits stop the assistant from following them.
  • A rule-breaking scraper. A scripted bot goes for admin pages and private data, then floods the site. SARA blocks it and keeps its deposit, and the owner sees why.

What's real and what isn't

Real

  • PayPal Sandbox payments: Orders v2 with authorization holds, captures, voids and refunds; Subscriptions; Vault; webhooks; JS SDK v6 buttons.
  • The AI: the assistant, the purchase check, the explanations and the owner's assistants call a real model.
  • Verify with PayPal reads the payment back through the PayPal MCP server.
  • Verified agents sign requests with Web Bot Auth (RFC 9421, Ed25519).

Made up for the demo

  • Sentinel News and its articles.
  • The rule-breaking scraper, which follows a script so it misbehaves the same way every time.
  • Sandbox accounts: a business account receives the money, and a personal account approves subscriptions. No real money moves.
