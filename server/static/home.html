Three steps to an agent-ready website

  1. 1

    Put SARA in front of your site

    Add your website and point its domain at the gateway. People reach your pages exactly as before; AI agents are recognised and gated.

  2. 2

    Set the price and the rules

    Choose the deposit per agent session, the paths agents may read, the ones they may not, and the rate limit. Your robots.txt is imported and enforced.

  3. 3

    Agents pay, or get blocked

    Unpaid agents get HTTP 402 and a way to pay. Forbidden paths get 403, floods get 429, and every decision comes with a plain-English AI explanation.

Why the money runs on PayPal

A deposit is a PayPal authorization hold. An honest agent's hold is voided, so it never pays a cent. A rule-breaker's hold is captured and lands in your PayPal account. Every decision can be checked against PayPal's own records.
