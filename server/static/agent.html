Behind the scenes

What the assistant can't show you: SARA and PayPal at work.

Aria knows nothing about this website in advance. Here you can follow what happens as it works: the website's SARA gateway sets the rules and the prices, PayPal moves the money, and your own limits decide what the assistant may buy.

What happens, step by step

Waiting for your request
  1. 1

    The assistant finds out what the site offers

    SARA publishes a machine-readable catalog at /.well-known/agent-commerce.json: deposit prices, pay-per-access items, subscriptions, and products. Any agent can read it; no screen-scraping.

  2. 2

    The site answers 402 Payment Required, with its rules

    Protected pages don't just open. SARA replies with the site's rules (allowed paths from robots.txt, rate limit, pages per session) and a refundable deposit priced by content: $5 public, $10 standard, $20 premium.

  3. 3

    The deposit is put on hold through PayPal

    The assistant's deposit becomes a PayPal hold (or a refundable payment). It's money on the line: the assistant gets it back only if it follows the rules.

  4. 4

    Every request is checked against the rules

    SARA checks each page request: is the path allowed, is it within the rate limit and the page budget? A rule-breaker is stopped with 403 or 429 and loses its deposit.

  5. 5

    Purchases go through PayPal, within your limits

    Videos, subscriptions, and products are PayPal orders priced by the site. Your limits are enforced in code: small purchases go through, anything above your limit or recurring asks you first. A second AI checks every purchase against what you asked for.

    • 6

      The session closes and the deposit is settled

      Rules followed: the PayPal hold is released, so nothing was ever charged. Rules broken: the site keeps the deposit, and an AI explanation cites the audit log.
