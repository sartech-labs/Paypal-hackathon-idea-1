SARA

Refundable PayPal deposits that keep AI crawlers accountable

An HTTP 402 gateway: AI agents pay a small refundable PayPal deposit to crawl. Follow the rules and get it back. Break them and lose it.

PayPal Orders v2 · Payments v2 · MCP server AI agent · explanations · PayPal assistant PayPal AI Hackathon 2026

Other 402 schemes charge for access. SARA charges for misbehaviour.

Compliant agent: deposit refunded, crawls free
Rogue agent: blocked, deposit forfeited

The money decision comes from deterministic policy rules. The AI acts, explains, and checks PayPal's records, but never decides who gets paid.
